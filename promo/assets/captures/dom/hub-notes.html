A submodule is a second repository pinned to one commit. The superproject records which commit; the submodule’s own working tree is whatever you left it at. Updating moves the working tree to the pinned commit — it does not change what is pinned.

  • This repository declares no submodules

Bisect finds the commit that introduced something by checking out the middle of a range and asking whether it is good or bad. The session lives in the repository, so it survives closing the app — and has to be reset rather than abandoned.

A note is text attached to a commit afterwards. It lives in its own ref, so writing one does not rewrite history — and it does not travel with an ordinary push, which is why fetching and pushing notes are separate actions here. Most hosting sites do not display notes at all.

1 commit(s) carry a note. They are marked in the history list; open one to read or edit it.
